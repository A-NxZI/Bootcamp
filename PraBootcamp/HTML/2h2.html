The Gift of the Magi

Chapter 1

One dollar and eighty-seven cents was all that Della had saved to buy a Christmas present for her beloved husband,
Jim. Anxious and determined to give him something worthy, she decided to sell her most prized possession: her long,
beautiful hair. With the twenty dollars she received from the shop, she eagerly searched the stores until she found
the perfect gift—an elegant platinum fob chain for Jim’s cherished gold watch, which had belonged to his father and
grandfather.

Chapter 2

When Jim arrived home, he stared at Della in stunned silence, not out of anger or disapproval, but out of sheer irony.
He slowly unwrapped his gift for her: a set of beautiful tortoiseshell combs she had long admired,1 purchased by selling
his own gold watch. Though their expensive gifts were now temporarily useless, they realized that their willingness
to sacrifice their greatest treasures for one another was the most valuable expression of love they could ever give.

1 "tortoiseshell combs"
